# Image assets

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for a fictional portfolio invitation. No external event photographs, real couple portraits or real venue claims.

- `assets/table.webp`: portrait editorial photograph of a sunlit courtyard table, ivory linen, white porcelain, brushed-gold cutlery, water glasses, white cosmos and roses, olive branches and an emerald ribbon; natural film-like light, no people, text, brands, religious books or alcohol. Used as the hero.
- `assets/paper.webp`: landscape editorial still life of blank handmade ivory envelopes on limestone, emerald silk ribbon, plain antique-gold wax seal, olive branch and white cosmos; natural sunlight, tactile paper, no text or logos. Used beside the programme.

The final prompts specified warm ivory, deep emerald, subtle gold, photorealistic-natural styling and no real event identity. PNG outputs were compressed/resized to WebP without changing their content. No remote image requests at runtime. Fonts are bundled with their individual OFL licence notices.
